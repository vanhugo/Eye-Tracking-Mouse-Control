**Eye Tracking Mouse Control**

Control your cursor using only your eyes!

**About the Project**

We built a hands-free mouse control system with computer vision. It uses a webcam to track eye movements and translate them into mouse cursor movements, allowing users to interact with their computers without physically moving a mouse. The project explores how computer vision can make technology more accessible, particularly for individuals with limited motor control or physical disabilities.

**Features**

Eye Tracking: Uses facial and iris landmarks to estimate eye movements.  
Mouse Control: Maps eye movements to on-screen cursor movements.  
Dwell-to-Click: Automatically clicks when the cursor stays in one position for 1.5 seconds.  
Calibration: Guides users through a calibration process to help map eye movements to screen coordinates.  
Webcam-Based: Uses a webcam to track eye movements without specialized eye-tracking hardware.

**How to Use**

After running the program make sure you're in full screen on the window that pops up. Follow the moving red dots by holding your head still and only moving your eyes. Once calibration is complete, move your eyes to control the mouse cursor. If the cursor does not respond as expected, try gently adjusting your head position until tracking works correctly. To trigger a click, keep your gaze on a position until the cursor remains stationary for approximately 1.5 seconds. To quit the program the webcam window must be open and then press 'q'.
